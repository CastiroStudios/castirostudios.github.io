Cast.iro Studios ("Cast.iro," "we," "us," or "our") is a video editing studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We've kept it specific to what this site actually does, it doesn't describe tools or trackers we don't use.

In short: this site does not run advertising or analytics trackers. The only optional technology is Google Fonts, and it only loads after you accept it in the cookie banner. We collect information you choose to send us directly, such as an email you write to apply for a project package.

1. Who we are

Cast.iro Studios is a post production and video editing service operating out of Dhaka Division, Bangladesh, serving clients globally on a remote basis. For any privacy question, you can reach us at castirostudios@gmail.com.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers the website you're currently reading (the pages that make up cast.iro's marketing site and project package pricing page). It does not cover:

  • Third party services we link to but don't operate, such as Instagram, Facebook and YouTube, each has its own privacy policy that applies once you leave our site, and third-party services used during a project have their own privacy policies.
  • Email correspondence exchanged through your own email provider (e.g. Gmail, Outlook) once you send us a message, that provider's privacy policy governs how it handles your outgoing mail;
  • Any separate contract, invoice, or file sharing tool we may use with you once a project package actually begins, which will be covered by the terms of that specific engagement.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you give us directly

We only receive personal information when you choose to send it to us. That happens when you:

  • Email us directly at castirostudios@gmail.com, or use an email link on this site (for example, the “Email the studio” button on our project pricing page). This opens your own email app and sends us your name, email address and whatever you choose to write. The project calculator itself does not submit anything;
  • Arrange a call directly with us, sharing the contact and scheduling details needed for that call.
  • Later, if we start working together, share raw footage, briefs, brand assets, or other project material with us through a channel we agree on together (for example, a shared drive).

3.2 Information collected automatically

This site itself does not run analytics or advertising trackers, so we do not automatically collect browsing behavior, device fingerprints, or ad identifiers. Our web hosting provider may, as a standard part of operating any website, keep basic server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) for security and reliability purposes. We do not access or use these logs for marketing.

3.3 Information from cookies and local storage

See Section 4 below, this covers the single piece of local storage this site uses, and how Google Fonts fits in.

4. Cookies & similar technology

This site does not set any advertising or analytics cookies. It uses two small pieces of technology, described honestly below:

  • Consent preference (localStorage): when you make a choice in the cookie banner, we store that single choice ("accept all" or "essential only") in your browser's local storage, under the key castiro-cookie-consent. This is strictly necessary to remember your preference and never leaves your browser, we never see it, and it contains no personal information.
  • Google Fonts: this site uses custom typefaces served by Google Fonts. If you choose "Accept all" in the cookie banner, your browser will request font files from Google's servers, which will receive your IP address as a normal part of that request, per Google's Privacy Policy. If you choose "Essential only," your browser instead uses your device's built in system fonts, and no request is ever sent to Google.

You can change your choice at any time using the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer of every page.

5. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and project package applications;
  • Schedule and conduct discovery calls;
  • Deliver the editing services you've engaged us for, once a project begins;
  • Keep the site secure and functioning correctly (server logs, consent preference); and
  • Meet legal or accounting obligations, such as retaining invoices.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Our legal basis for processing

Where privacy laws that require a stated legal basis apply to you (for example, the EU/UK GDPR), we rely on: your consent (for optional technology like Google Fonts), the steps necessary to enter into or perform a contract with you (when you apply for or engage a project package/project), and our legitimate interest in running a secure, functioning website and responding to enquiries sent to us.

7. When we share information

We do not sell your information. We may share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate (for example, our website hosting provider), each bound by their own privacy terms;
  • Team members or subcontractors directly involved in delivering a project you've engaged us for, and only to the extent needed to do the work;
  • Authorities, if required to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or to protect our rights.

8. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and application emails for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and, if a project begins, for the duration of that engagement plus a reasonable period afterward for accounting and legal purposes (typically up to 6 years, consistent with standard bookkeeping practice). You can ask us to delete correspondence that isn't needed for these purposes at any time, see Section 11.

9. How we protect it

We take reasonable technical and organizational precautions to protect information sent to us, for example, limiting who has access to client files and correspondence. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security, but we do not knowingly leave client information exposed.

10. International visitors

We're based in Bangladesh and work with clients worldwide. If you contact us or engage our services from outside Bangladesh, your information may be processed in Bangladesh or in the country where a third party service we use (such as Google) operates its servers, which may have different data protection laws than your home country.

11. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain uses of it. You can exercise any of these by emailing castirostudios@gmail.com, we'll respond within a reasonable time, generally within 30 days. You can also change your cookie choice at any time via the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer.

12. Children's privacy

This site and our services are intended for businesses, creators, and individuals who are at least 18 years old, or the age of majority in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we start using a new tool on the site. If we make a material change, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to check back periodically.

14. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, email us at castirostudios@gmail.com. This policy is governed by the laws of Bangladesh, consistent with our Terms & Conditions.
