This Refund Policy explains when you can and can't get your money back for a Cast.iro Studios project package plan or one off project. It should be read together with our Terms & Conditions, which it forms a part of.

In short: once our editors have started work on your project, that payment is final. If you cancel before any work has started, you're entitled to a full refund. Billing mistakes and duplicate charges are always refunded in full.

1. Overview

Video editing is time intensive, hands on creative work, once an editor picks up your brief and starts cutting, that time and capacity is spent regardless of what happens afterward. Our refund policy reflects that: refunds are available before work begins, and are not available once it has, except in the specific situations described below.

2. Free / trial tier

Our free sample is $0, so there is nothing to refund. If you're unhappy with a trial edit, tell us, we'll use that feedback, but no payment is involved to reverse.

3. Project packages

For paid project packages (1, 10 or 20 videos):

  • If you cancel before we've assigned an editor or started any work on your current project, you're entitled to a full refund of that project's payment.
  • Once work has started on a project (meaning an editor has begun a brief, cut, or revision for that project), that project's payment is non refundable, even if you use fewer edits than your plan allows.
  • Packages are one-time purchases and do not renew automatically. See Section 6.

4. One off projects

For one off projects quoted and agreed outside a project package:

  • If you cancel before any editing work has begun, you're entitled to a full refund of any deposit or payment made.
  • If you cancel after work has begun, payment already made for that project is non refundable, reflecting the time and capacity already committed. If you've paid in installments, any installment not yet due is simply not collected.

5. What's never refundable

Regardless of plan or project type, the following are not eligible for a refund:

  • Change of mind after a completed, delivered edit that matches the agreed brief;
  • Delays caused by you providing footage, briefs, or feedback late: turnaround targets assume timely input on your end;
  • Dissatisfaction with a creative direction you approved during the review process; and
  • Any project phase where editing work has already started, as described above.

6. Cancelling a project package

Contact the studio through the channel used to agree your project to request cancellation. Packages do not automatically renew. Refund eligibility follows the provisions above and applicable legal requirements.

7. Exceptions we'll always honor

We'll issue a full refund, regardless of whether work has started, if:

  • You were charged twice for the same project by mistake;
  • You were billed an incorrect amount due to an error on our part; or
  • We are unable to deliver the service we agreed to, for reasons within our control.

Outside of these situations, refund requests are reviewed case by case, at our discretion, where the circumstances clearly fall outside normal project delays or creative disagreements covered above.

8. How to request a refund

Email castirostudios@gmail.com with your name, the plan or project in question, the payment date, and the reason for your request. We aim to respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method and may take additional time to appear, depending on your bank or payment provider.

9. Chargebacks

If you believe you've been charged in error, please contact us first, most issues can be resolved directly and faster than through a chargeback. Filing a chargeback for work that was delivered as agreed may result in a pause of ongoing services while the dispute is resolved.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. The policy in effect at the time of your payment applies to that payment.

11. Contact us

Questions about a refund or this policy can be sent to castirostudios@gmail.com. This policy is governed by the laws of Bangladesh, consistent with our Terms & Conditions.
