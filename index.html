Good questions.
Clear answers.

A little clarity before your next creative collaboration.

What should I send to get started?

Send a short outline of your project, your target platform, a reference you like, and your deadline. Include a link to your footage if it is ready. We will confirm the scope before work begins.

Can we start with a single edit?

Yes. A single video starts at $10, 10 videos at $50, and 20 videos at $100. These are one-time project prices for standard editing. You can also select a free sample on our project pricing page.

How quickly can I get my first cut?

We confirm timing after reviewing the number of videos, duration, required production and supplied assets. Tell us your deadline in the project brief.

How do feedback and revisions work?

Share consolidated, timestamped feedback. Revision allowances and additional work are agreed in the project quote before editing begins.

Can you adapt one edit for different platforms?

Yes. Tell us where each version will run and which aspect ratios you need. We can plan platform-specific cuts, captions and pacing in your project quote.
